01 · About the club

A student community built around the future of AI.

The Agentic AI Club brings Roux Institute students together to explore what happens when AI systems can reason, plan, use tools, and take action.

We create opportunities for students to learn from one another, experiment with emerging technologies, and collaborate on projects that turn ideas into working systems.
