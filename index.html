About the Agentic AI Club

A student-led community for exploring, experimenting with and building practical systems around agentic AI.

  • Roux Institute, Portland
  • Open to students
  • Curiosity welcome
01 · Who we are

A place to explore what comes after chat.

The Agentic AI Club brings students together around a rapidly changing area of artificial intelligence: systems that can reason through tasks, use tools, interact with their environment and work toward goals.

The club is a space for students to learn from each other, test ideas, share experiments and build practical projects. Participation can range from attending a discussion to contributing to a hands-on project.

02 · Participation

Learn by taking part.

The club is designed around active participation rather than passive learning. Students can bring questions, ideas, technical skills or simply curiosity.

  • 01

    Explore

    Discuss new agent architectures, tools, research and practical use cases.

  • 02

    Experiment

    Try ideas through workshops, small prototypes and technical sessions.

  • 03

    Build

    Collaborate on projects that turn concepts into working systems.

03 · Our focus

From ideas to useful systems.

We are interested in the practical side of agentic AI: how agents are designed, how they use tools, how multiple steps can be coordinated, and where these systems can create meaningful value.

Our focus can evolve with the community. As students bring new interests and projects, the club can become a place to explore the questions that matter most to them.

How club projects will work

A simple path from idea to something you can show people.

  1. 01

    Pitch an idea

    Any member can pitch. A short description of the problem and a rough plan is enough to start.

  2. 02

    Form a team

    Ideas that find a few interested members become club projects.

  3. 03

    Build together

    Teams meet regularly, share progress with the club and get help when they're stuck.

  4. 04

    Show it off

    Finished projects are demoed to the club and featured on this page.

Common questions

Can't find what you need? Ask us directly.

Do I need AI or coding experience?

No. The club is for anyone curious about AI agents, whether you've built one before or are just getting started. Events are designed so you can follow along from scratch.

Is there a membership fee?

No. Membership and events are free.

How much time does it take?

As much as you want to give it. Come to the events that interest you, and join a project team if and when you want to go deeper.

Can students from other Northeastern campuses join?

Yes. Events are held at the Roux Institute in Portland, but anyone from Northeastern is welcome to sign up and get involved.

Can I join partway through the semester?

Any time. Sign up whenever you're ready and come to the next event.
