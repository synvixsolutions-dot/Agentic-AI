Join the Club

Membership is free and open to every Roux Institute student, whatever your program or background. If you're curious about AI agents, you'll fit in.

  • Free, no dues
  • No experience required
  • Sign-up takes about 3 minutes

What you get as a member

Show up as often as you like. Here's what's waiting when you do.

  1. 01

    Build real things

    Work on projects that ship, with teammates who care about getting them right.

  2. 02

    Learn from practitioners

    Workshops and talks on how agents are designed, built and tested in practice.

  3. 03

    Find teammates

    Meet students across programs and find people for class projects, hackathons and co-ops.

  4. 04

    Show your work

    Club projects give you something concrete to point to on your résumé and in interviews.

Common questions

Can't find what you need? Ask us directly.

Do I need AI or coding experience?

No. The club is for anyone curious about AI agents, whether you've built one before or are just getting started. Events are designed so you can follow along from scratch.

Is there a membership fee?

No. Membership and events are free.

How much time does it take?

As much as you want to give it. Come to the events that interest you, and join a project team if and when you want to go deeper.

Can students from other Northeastern campuses join?

Yes. Events are held at the Roux Institute in Portland, but anyone from Northeastern is welcome to sign up and get involved.

Can I join partway through the semester?

Any time. Sign up whenever you're ready and come to the next event.
